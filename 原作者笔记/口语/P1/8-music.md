1. **Do you prefer sad or happy music?**
	
	To be honest, I hardly ever listen to music in my daily life. But when I go running, I really enjoy energetic and powerful songs. This kind of music can cheer me up and help me keep going, especially when I feel tired and want to give up halfway. 
	
	说实话，我平日里几乎不怎么听音乐。但跑步的时候，我格外喜欢激昂、充满力量感的歌曲。这种音乐能鼓舞我、让我坚持下去，特别是当我感到疲惫、想要半途而废的时候。

2. **Does happy music make you feel more excited?**

	Yes, it does. Upbeat songs always put me in a better mood, and I feel much more energetic afterwards. That’s probably why I prefer happy music to sad music — I’d rather feel motivated than sentimental.

	会。节奏欢快的歌总能让我心情变好，听完之后整个人都更有精神。可能也是因为这个，比起伤感的音乐，我更喜欢欢快的——我更想要动力，而不是多愁善感。

3. - Have you taken any music classes?

	No, I haven’t taken any music lessons at all. Actually, I know nothing about music. Whenever I sing, I sound really bad.
	
	没有，我一节音乐课都没上过。事实上，我对音乐一窍不通，每次唱歌，我的嗓音都很难听。

4. **Do you listen to music while doing other things?**

	Rarely, to be honest. I find it hard to concentrate when there’s music playing, so I usually keep it off while I’m studying. The only time I listen to music is when I go running, because it helps me keep going.

	说实话，很少。有音乐的时候我很难集中注意力，所以学习时一般不开音乐。我唯一会听音乐的时候就是跑步，因为音乐能让我坚持跑下去。
