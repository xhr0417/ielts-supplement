## 一、答题原则与五类问题

> 先直接回答问题，再选择相关的理由展开。科技、生活压力、健康等可以提供思路；与问题无关时，不必使用。可以用“回答＋理由＋例子”练习，不要求每次机械凑齐。

P3 常见五类问法、开头句型与简短示范：

| 问题类型 | 常见问法 | 常用开头 | 简短完整回答示范 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **原因类** | Why...? / What causes...? | The main reason is that... / This is mainly because... | **Q: Why do many young people like shopping online?**<br>I think the main reason is convenience. People can compare prices and order things on their phones in a few minutes instead of going to a crowded mall. Besides, online stores often offer discounts, which is attractive to students and young workers. |
| **影响类** | What are the effects of...? | This can lead to... / One major impact is that... | **Q: What effects does working long hours have on people?**<br>Working long hours can cause both physical and mental health problems. When people sit in the office all day, they lack exercise and sleep, and they hardly have time to relax with their families. Over time, this heavy pressure makes them feel exhausted. |
| **对比类** | How is A different from B? | The main difference is that A..., while B... / Unlike A, B... | **Q: What is the difference between watching movies at home and in a cinema?**<br>The main difference is the atmosphere and cost. Watching a movie in a cinema gives you a big screen and great sound effects, which is exciting for action films. By contrast, watching at home is much cheaper and more comfortable because you can pause whenever you want. |
| **解决类** | How can we solve...? / What should be done? | Both individuals and the government can... / A practical way is to... | **Q: How can we encourage people to produce less household waste?**<br>Both the government and individuals need to take action. Local governments can set up clear waste-sorting rules and charge for plastic bags. At the same time, individuals can bring their own reusable bags when shopping and avoid buying things they do not really need. |
| **预测类** | Will this trend continue in the future? | I think it will probably..., because... / It depends, but... | **Q: Will paper books disappear in the future?**<br>I don't think they will disappear completely. Although e-books are convenient and cheap, many people still prefer the feeling of holding a real book and taking notes on paper. Also, reading paper books is much easier on the eyes after staring at screens all day. |

## 二、常用衔接与表态开头

| 场景 | 英文表达 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 谈到原因 | Well, I guess the main reason is that... | 我想主要原因是…… |
| 谈到影响 | In my view, this could lead to... | 在我看来，这可能会导致…… |
| 表示同意 | I agree with that, mainly because... | 我同意这一点，主要是因为…… |
| 分情况回答 | It depends on the situation. For example, if... | 这看情况。比如，如果…… |
| 给出建议 | I believe both governments and individuals should... | 我认为政府和个人都应该…… |
| 举例说明 | Take... for example. / For instance,... | 以……为例。 |
| 补充另一面 | On the other hand, we should not ignore... | 另一方面，我们也不应忽视…… |

## 三、八组高频话题语料（按相关性选用，已校订）

| 英文句子 | 中文对照 |
|---|---|
| **1. 生活成本高 high living costs** | |
| Nowadays, ==living costs are getting higher and higher.== | 如今生活成本越来越高。 |
| Young people face heavy pressure from daily spending. | 年轻人面临日常开销带来的巨大压力。 |
| Housing and food take up most of people's salary. | 房租和食物花掉人们大部分工资。 |
| **2. 生活节奏快 fast pace of life** | |
| Modern city people ==live at a really fast pace.== | 现代城市居民生活节奏很快。 |
| People are ==always busy== and ==have little free time.== | 人们总是很忙碌，几乎没有空闲时间。 |
| They ==go to work in a hurry every day== and ==almost never relax.== | 他们每天匆忙上班，很少放松自己。 |
| **3. 健康问题 health problems** | |
| ==Living at a fast pace== brings many ==physical health problems.== | 快节奏生活带来很多身体健康问题。 |
| Many people ==lack exercise and sleep== every day. | 很多人每天缺乏运动和睡眠。 |
| ==Scrolling through social media for a long time== can also cause ==mental stress.== | 长时间刷社交媒体也会造成精神压力。 |
| Sitting for long hours without exercise leads to physical problems. | 长时间久坐不运动会带来身体问题。 |
| **4. 社交与沟通 social communication** | |
| People ==spend too much time online== and ==ignore face-to-face talks.== | 人们花太多时间上网，忽略面对面交流。 |
| Some people become less confident in ==real-life communication.== | 有些人在现实社交中变得不够自信。 |
| **5. 科技与便利 technology & convenience** | |
| Most people choose new technology ==just for convenience.== | 大多数人选择新技术主要是为了方便。 |
| People prefer ==easy and fast ways== to finish daily tasks. | 人们更喜欢用简单快捷的方式处理日常事务。 |
| The internet has changed the way we live and work. | 互联网改变了我们生活和工作的方式。 |
| **6. AI 与工作 AI & jobs** | |
| AI can handle many ==repeated and boring tasks== for humans. | AI 可以帮人类处理许多重复枯燥的工作。 |
| Some workers worry AI will ==take their jobs== in the future. | 一些劳动者担心未来 AI 会取代他们的工作。 |
| **7. 信息过载 online information** | |
| We ==are flooded with== all kinds of information online every day. | 我们每天接触大量网络信息。 |
| It is sometimes hard for people to ==sort out reliable information online.== | 人们有时很难分辨网上的可靠信息。 |
| **8. 消费与广告 consumerism & ads** | |
| ==Consumerism== pushes people to buy things they don't really need in daily life. | 消费主义促使人们购买日常并不真正需要的东西。 |
| Many people shop ==just for fun== ==instead of real demand.== | 很多人购物只是为了消遣，而非实际需求。 |
| Businesses ==use advertisements== to encourage people to ==spend more money.== | 商家利用广告鼓励人们多消费。 |
| **9. 教育与成长 education & skills** | |
| It is important for children to learn both academic knowledge and practical life skills. | 对孩子来说，学习书本知识和生活技能都很重要。 |
| **10. 环境与公共意识 environment & public awareness** | |
| Raising public awareness is the first step to solving environmental problems. | 提高公众意识是解决环境问题的第一步。 |

## 四、考前速练建议

1. **先练直接破题**：遮住上面示范的英文，看五类问题，第一句话必须在 3 秒内直接回答问题（不要绕弯子）。
2. **选 1–2 个贴题理由**：只有当题目真的涉及工作、消费、科技、健康时，才调用第三部分的对应句子。
3. **录音自查（30–45 秒/题）**：重点听第一句有没有直接答到点子上，以及第三人称单数、过去时是否准确。
