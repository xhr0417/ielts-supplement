## 一、审题与结构要点

双边讨论（Discuss both views and give your own opinion）要求**同时讨论双方观点，并明确给出你自己的看法**：

| 段落 | 核心任务 | 常见丢分点提醒 |
|---|---|---|
| **开头段** | 改写双方观点 + **直接亮明你的立场** | 不要只写 `present my own opinion` 却不表态，考官希望在开头就能看到你的立场 |
| **主体段 1** | 讨论你不那么倾向的一方（为什么有人这样想） | 要写出对方合理的逻辑，不能一上来就批驳，否则不算真正讨论了双方观点 |
| **主体段 2** | 讨论你支持的一方（为什么这一方更有道理） | 理由与例子要具体，可自然承接个人立场 |
| **结尾段** | 总结双方 + 再次重申你的立场与核心原因 | 注意语法：**`that` 从句后面不要再加 `is more reasonable`**，避免双谓语错误 |

## 二、常用句型模板（已修正语法陷阱）

Some people think public transport should be free to everyone, while many others think it would be a heavy financial burden. ==Discuss both views and give your own opinion.==
一部分人认为公共交通应当向所有人免费，然而另有许多人认为这会造成沉重负担。讨论双方观点并给出你自己的看法。

| 英文模板 | 中文翻译 |
| --- | --- |
| Opinions vary greatly on whether... | 关于是否应当……，人们观点不一。 |
| Some people believe that..., while others argue that... | 一部分人认为……，而另一部分人主张…… |
| This essay will examine both perspectives, and I personally side with the latter / former view. | 本文将讨论双方观点，我个人更倾向于后一种 / 前一种看法。 |
|  |  |
| On the one hand, those who support the former view hold the valid reason that... | 一方面，支持前者观点的人有合理的理由认为…… |
| Since..., it is beneficial for... to... | 由于……，这有利于…… 去…… |
| This can help people improve their ability in... | 这能帮助人们提升…… 方面的能力。 |
| One case in point is that... | 一个典型的例子是…… |
|  |  |
| On the other hand, opponents of this view claim that... | 另一方面，持不同看法的人认为…… |
| As..., it may lead to negative consequences for... | 由于……，这可能会给…… 带来负面影响。 |
| Rather than solving the problem, this approach may cause issues such as... | 这种做法不仅无法解决问题，还可能引发…… 等问题。 |
| For instance,... | 举例来说，…… |
|  |  |
| Overall, there are valid arguments on both sides. | 总体来看，双方都存在合理的论点。 |
| **写法 A（接完整句子）**：From my perspective, I am more convinced that + **完整从句**. | 在我看来，我更认同……（后接完整句子，**句末不要再加 is more reasonable**）。 |
| **写法 B（接名词短语）**：From my perspective, the latter / former view is more reasonable. | 在我看来，后一种 / 前一种观点更为合理。 |
| This is because it can not only..., but also... in the long term. | 这是因为从长远来看，它不仅可以……，还能够…… |

## 三、示范题目与参考习作

Some people think online reading will replace printed books and newspapers in the future, while others believe printed media will always exist. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
有人认为未来网络阅读会取代纸质书报，另一些人则认为纸质媒介不会消失。讨论双方观点并给出自己看法。

---

==Opinions vary greatly on whether== traditional printed books and newspapers will be completely replaced by online reading in the future. ==Some people believe that== digital reading will dominate people's daily lives, ==while others argue that== printed publications still have irreplaceable value. ==This essay will examine both perspectives, and I personally agree with the latter view.==

**翻译**：关于传统纸质书报未来是否会被网络阅读彻底取代，人们观点不一。一部分人认为数字化阅读将主导人们的日常生活，而另一部分人主张纸质读物仍有不可替代的价值。本文将讨论双方观点，我个人赞同后一种看法。

---

==On the one hand, those who support the former view hold the valid reason that== online reading resources are more convenient and affordable for the public. ==Since== massive reading materials can be accessed anytime and anywhere on the Internet, ==it is beneficial for== ordinary people ==to== gain information at a very low cost. ==This can greatly help== readers ==absorb== new information efficiently. ==A typical example is that== numerous news articles and e-books are available online for free, which has significantly reduced people's reliance on physical books.

**翻译**：一方面，支持前者观点的人有合理理由认为，网络阅读资源对大众而言更加便捷实惠。由于人们可以随时随地在网上获取海量阅读资料，这有利于普通人低成本获取信息，高效吸收新知。典型的例子是，大量新闻与电子书可在线免费阅览，大幅降低了人们对实体书的依赖。

---

==On the other hand, proponents of printed media claim that== physical books and newspapers offer a unique reading experience and collection value. ==As== screen reading often causes eye strain and constant distractions, ==it may lead to negative consequences for== people's reading quality and concentration. ==For instance,== many online readers tend to skim quickly and get interrupted by pop-up notifications, whereas printed books encourage people to focus and think deeply.

**翻译**：另一方面，支持纸质媒介的人认为，实体书报拥有独特的阅读体验与收藏价值。由于屏幕阅读容易造成视疲劳和频繁分心，这会对阅读质量和专注力带来负面影响。举例来说，许多网络读者习惯快速浏览，还容易被弹窗通知打断，而纸质书更能让人专注精读和深度思考。

---

==Overall, there are valid arguments on both sides.== ==From my perspective, I am more convinced that== printed media will never be fully replaced by online reading. ==This is because it can not only== provide a more focused reading experience, ==but also== preserve cultural heritage in the long term.

**翻译**：总体而言，双方观点都有合理之处。在我看来，我更认同纸质媒介不会被网络阅读彻底取代。这是因为从长远来看，纸质读物不仅能提供更专注的阅读体验，还能更好地传承文化。
