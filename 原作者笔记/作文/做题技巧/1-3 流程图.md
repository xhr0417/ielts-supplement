## 开头

The flow chart illustrates the process of manufacturing 【成品】 from 【原料】.
该流程图展示了由【原料】生产【成品】的工序流程。
## 概述

==It is noticeable that there are seven distinct stages== in the manufacturing process of 【成品】, beginning with 【第一步】 and ending with 【最后一步】.
值得注意的是，整个制造流程分为 7 个明显的阶段，从【第一步】开始，以【最后一步】结束。

==These stages mainly include== raw material ==preparation==, fibre ==processing==, and fabric ==production.==
这些阶段主要包括原料准备、纤维加工和成品生产。
