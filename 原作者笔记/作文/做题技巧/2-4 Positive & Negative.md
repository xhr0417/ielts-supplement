## 一、审题与结构要点

积极还是消极（Is this a positive or negative development?）考察你对**某种社会趋势或现象**的价值判断：

| 审题检查项 | 注意事项与示例 |
|---|---|
| **1. 看清受影响的对象** | 注意题干问的是对**个人（individuals）**、**家庭（families）**还是**社会（society）**的影响。例如真题：*Newspapers have a huge influence on young people's lives. Is it a positive or negative effect on society?* 既要谈对年轻人的影响，也要扣住对社会的整体影响。 |
| **2. 立场要鲜明** | 可以写“整体积极（利大于弊）”或“整体消极（弊大于利）”，开头直接给出判断。 |
| **3. 让步段为可选** | 下方模板的“2 个正面理由 + 1 个负面让步”结构很稳妥，但第三段让步段并非强制；如果两段同向理由已经展开得很充分，写四段（开头 + 两段主体 + 结尾）完全可以。 |

## 三、例题与参考习作

Nowadays, many young people choose to study abroad. Is this a positive or negative development?
现如今，许多年轻人选择出国留学。这一现象是积极的还是消极的？

---

==It is widely observed that== an increasing number of young people choose to pursue further education in foreign countries. ==From my perspective, this trend brings more advantages than disadvantages== for young students.

**翻译**：如今人们普遍注意到，越来越多的年轻人选择出国深造。在我看来，这一趋势对青年学生而言利大于弊。

---

==First and foremost, the most obvious benefit of this phenomenon is that== studying abroad helps students improve their academic ability and career competitiveness. ==Since== many overseas universities have advanced teaching resources and research platforms, ==it can contribute to== students' professional development in their fields. ==This is particularly true for students== who major in science and engineering, ==as== they can gain access to cutting-edge technology and experienced scholars. ==For example,== many engineering graduates return with stronger research skills after studying abroad, which makes them competitive in the job market.

**翻译**：首先，这一现象最显著的好处是，出国留学有助于学生提升学术能力与就业竞争力。由于许多海外高校拥有先进的教学资源与科研平台，这能够助力学生在自身领域获得专业成长。这一点对于理工科学生尤为适用，因为他们可以接触前沿技术与资深学者。例如，许多工科留学生毕业后科研能力显著提升，在就业市场上很有竞争力。

---

==In addition, another positive effect of this trend is that== overseas study cultivates students' independence and broadens their horizons. ==Because== students have to handle daily life and academic problems alone in a foreign country, ==it enables them to== develop better problem-solving and cross-cultural communication skills. ==Instead of== staying in a familiar environment and relying on their parents, young people can experience diverse cultures and lifestyles. ==For instance,== students living abroad often learn to manage their budget, arrange schedules, and deal with cultural differences independently.

**翻译**：此外，这一趋势的另一项积极影响是，留学会培养学生的独立性、拓宽其视野。由于学生在异国必须独自处理生活与学业问题，这能够锻炼他们解决问题与跨文化沟通的能力。相比于待在熟悉的环境里依赖父母，年轻人可以体验多元的文化与生活方式。举例来说，在海外生活的学生通常能学会独立管理开支、规划时间、应对文化差异。

---

==However, we should not ignore the potential downsides of this trend.== ==Although this phenomenon may be beneficial to== students' long-term development ==in many ways, it can also bring challenges to== their mental well-being and family finances. ==Specifically,== many young students suffer from loneliness and **culture shock** when they first arrive in a foreign country. ==One case in point is that== some students struggle to adapt to the new teaching style and living environment, which leads to anxiety and poor academic performance.

**翻译**：然而，我们也不应忽视这一趋势潜在的消极面。尽管该现象在很多方面有利于学生的长远发展，它也会给学生的心理健康与家庭经济带来挑战。具体而言，很多年轻学生初到异国时会感到孤独、遭遇文化冲击。一个恰当的例子是，部分学生难以适应全新的教学模式与生活环境，进而产生焦虑情绪、学业表现下滑。

---

==In conclusion, although this trend may occasionally bring minor drawbacks such as== financial pressure and adaptation difficulties, ==I still believe that it is an overall positive development, as it can== broaden young people's horizons and enhance their future career prospects.

**翻译**：总而言之，尽管这一趋势偶尔会带来一些小弊端，例如经济压力与适应困难，我依旧认为这整体是积极的发展趋势，因为它能够拓宽年轻人的眼界并提升未来的职业前景。
