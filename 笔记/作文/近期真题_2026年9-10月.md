### Agree or disagree（19 道）

模板：[Agree or disagree 模板](<../../原作者笔记/作文/做题技巧/2-1 Agree or disagree.md>)

- **9/28–9/30** One long-distance flight consumes fuel which a car uses in several years' time, but they cause the same amount of pollution. So some people think that we should discourage non-essential flights, such as tourist travel, rather than limit the use of cars. To what extent do you agree or disagree? `近期重复` `往年旧题`
  - 一次长途飞行消耗的燃料相当于一辆车开好几年，造成的污染也相当。所以有人认为应该减少旅游这类非必要的飞行，而不是限制开车。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/21–9/27** Some people suggest that the government should spend money putting more works of art, like paintings and statues, into towns and cities to make them attractive places. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  - 有人建议政府花钱在城镇里放更多艺术品，比如绘画和雕塑，让城市更有吸引力。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/21–9/27** People who decide on a career path early in their lives and keep to it are more likely to have a satisfying working life than those who change jobs frequently. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  - 早早确定职业方向并坚持下去的人，比经常换工作的人更可能拥有满意的职业生活。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/21–9/27** Some people say it is more important to plant trees in the open spaces in towns and cities than to build more housing. To what extent do you agree or disagree? `往年旧题`
  - 有人说在城市的空地上种树比建更多住房更重要。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/21–9/27** Countries should try to produce all the food for the population and import as little food as possible. To what extent do you agree or disagree? `往年旧题`
  - 国家应该尽量自己生产全部粮食，尽可能少进口。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/14–9/20** The shopping habits of people depend more on their age group than on any other factors. To what extent do you agree or disagree? `近期重复` `往年旧题`
  - 人们的购物习惯更多取决于年龄段，而不是其他因素。你在多大程度上同意？
- **9/14–9/20** Some employers are giving increased importance to employing people with good social skills in addition to good qualifications. Do you agree or disagree that social skills are as important as good qualifications for success in a job? `近期重复` `往年旧题`

  - 大多数国家用监狱解决犯罪问题，但更实际的办法是给公众更好的教育。你在多大程度上同意？

### Discuss both views（5 道）

模板：[Discuss both views 模板](<../../原作者笔记/作文/做题技巧/2-2 Discuss both views.md>)

- **9/26–10/2** Because of modern technology, it is no longer necessary for people to learn handwriting skills, but others believe that handwriting is still an important skill. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  - 有人认为由于现代科技，人们不再需要学习书写技能；另一些人认为书写仍是一项重要技能。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。（考生回忆，措辞按常见题干整理）
- **9/28–9/30** Some people think that language should be taught in small classes, while other people think the number of students in a language class does not matter. Discuss both views and give your opinion. `往年旧题`
  - 有人认为语言应该小班教学，也有人认为语言课的人数无关紧要。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。
- **9/14–9/20** Some people argue that job satisfaction is more important than job security, while others believe that they cannot always expect job satisfaction and a permanent job is more important. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.
  - 有人认为工作满意度比工作稳定更重要，也有人认为不能总指望满意，一份稳定的工作更重要。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。
- **9/7–9/13** Some people say that the purpose of education is to prepare individuals to be useful to society. Others say its purpose is to prepare individuals to achieve personal ambitions. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. `往年旧题`
  - 有人说教育的目的是让人对社会有用，也有人说是帮助个人实现抱负。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。
- **9/1–9/6** Some believe that it is good for a country's culture to import films and TV programmes, while others think a country should have its own films and TV programmes. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. `往年旧题`
  - 有人认为进口影视节目对一个国家的文化有好处，也有人认为国家应该有自己的影视节目。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。

### Reasons / Problems & Solutions（2 道）

模板：[Reasons / Problems & Solutions](<做题技巧/2-3 Reasons & Solutions.md>)

- **9/14–9/20**（问题 + 解决办法） Natural resources such as oil, forests and freshwater are being consumed at an alarming rate all around the world. What problems does this cause? How can we solve these problems? `往年旧题`
  - 石油、森林、淡水等自然资源在全世界被以惊人的速度消耗。这会造成什么问题？怎么解决？
- **9/1–9/6**（原因 + 解决办法） In recent years, modern lifestyles and increasingly busy schedules have had a significant impact on people's eating habits, leading to serious health problems. What are the causes of this trend and what solutions can be implemented to address it?
  - 近年来，现代生活方式和越来越忙的日程极大影响了人们的饮食习惯，导致严重的健康问题。原因是什么？可以采取什么解决办法？

### Two-part questions（5 道）

模板：[Two-part questions](<做题技巧/2-5 Two-part questions.md>)

- **10/1–10/4**（原因 + 积极还是消极） These days more fathers stay at home and take care of their children while mothers go out to work. What could be the reasons for this? Is it a positive or negative development?
  - 如今越来越多父亲在家照顾孩子，母亲外出工作。原因可能是什么？这是积极还是消极的发展？
- **10/1–10/4**（原因 + 利是否大于弊） In the past, people used to travel to see the differences from their home country. However, the scenery in places around the world seems similar nowadays. What are the causes of these similarities? Do you think that the advantages of these similarities outweigh the disadvantages? `往年旧题`
  - 过去人们旅行是为了看和本国不同的东西，但如今世界各地的景色越来越相似。原因是什么？你认为这种相似利大于弊吗？
- **9/28–10/4**（有多重要 + 积极还是消极） Ambition is a positive quality for people to have in society today. How important is it for people who want to succeed in life? Is it a positive or negative characteristic? `近期重复` `往年旧题`
  - 进取心被认为是当今社会中的一种积极品质。它对想要成功的人有多重要？它是积极还是消极的特质？
- **10/1–10/4**（原因 + 影响） Today, people in many countries have to spend more and more time away from their families. Why is this happening? What effects does this have on the people themselves and on their families? `往年旧题`
  - 如今很多国家的人不得不花越来越多的时间离开家人。为什么会这样？这对他们本人和家人有什么影响？
- **9/1–9/6**（原因 + 积极还是消极） Fewer and fewer people today write by hand using a pen or pencil. What are the reasons for this? Is this a positive or a negative development? `往年旧题`
  - 如今用笔手写的人越来越少。原因是什么？这是积极还是消极的发展？
