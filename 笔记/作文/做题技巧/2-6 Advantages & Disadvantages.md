# 2-6 Advantages & Disadvantages（利弊题）

## 题型识别

| 问法 | 要做什么 |
|---|---|
| Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? / Do the benefits outweigh the drawbacks? | 必须表态：利大于弊，或弊大于利 |
| What are the advantages and disadvantages of …? | 利和弊各写一段，可以不表态，结尾给一句倾向更好 |

> 和 2-4 Positive & Negative 很像。2-4 问“这个趋势好不好”，这里问“好处是不是比坏处大”。结构可以几乎一样，区别在让步段：这里要说明坏处为什么比较小。

## 结构

| 段落 | 内容（以“利大于弊”为例） |
|---|---|
| 开头 | 改写题目 + 表态：利大于弊 |
| 主体段 1 | 第一个好处：观点 + 解释 + 例子 |
| 主体段 2 | 第二个好处：观点 + 解释 + 例子 |
| 主体段 3 | 承认坏处，再说明坏处比较小（短期的、可以解决、只影响少数人） |
| 结论 | 重申利大于弊，概括两个好处 |

> 如果认为弊大于利，把主体段 1、2 换成两个坏处，主体段 3 承认好处并说明好处有限。

| Since …, it allows sb to do sth. | 由于……，这让某人能够做某事。 |
| This is particularly true for … | 这一点对……尤其明显。 |
| For example, … | 例如，…… |
|  |  |
| Another benefit is that … | 另一个好处是…… |

| Instead of doing sth, sb can … | 某人不必……，而是可以…… |
| For instance, … | 举例来说，…… |

## 示例（2026 年 9 月下旬真题回忆）

**题目**：More and more students are choosing to study at colleges and universities in a foreign country. Do the benefits of studying abroad outweigh the drawbacks?
**译**：越来越多的学生选择去国外上大学。出国留学的好处大于坏处吗？

**开头**：It is increasingly common for young people to attend universities in other countries. In my view, the benefits of studying abroad clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
**译**：年轻人去国外读大学越来越普遍。在我看来，出国留学的好处明显大于坏处。

**主体段 1**：The most significant benefit is that students can receive a high-quality education. Since many overseas universities have excellent teachers and modern laboratories, they allow students to learn the latest knowledge in their field. This is particularly true for subjects such as engineering and medicine. For example, a student who studies computer science in the UK can take part in real research projects, which will make him more competitive when he looks for a job.
**译**：最大的好处是学生能接受高质量的教育。由于很多海外大学有优秀的老师和现代化的实验室，学生能学到本领域最新的知识。这一点对工程、医学这类专业尤其明显。例如，在英国读计算机的学生可以参与真实的科研项目，这会让他找工作时更有竞争力。

**主体段 2**：Another benefit is that studying abroad helps young people become more independent and open-minded. Because they live far away from their families, they have to cook, manage their money and solve problems on their own. Instead of only reading about other cultures, they can make friends from different countries and experience different ways of life. For instance, many international students learn to plan their budgets and deal with cultural differences within their first year.
**译**：另一个好处是，留学让年轻人更独立、更开放。因为远离家人，他们必须自己做饭、管钱、解决问题。他们不只是在书上了解其他文化，而是能结交不同国家的朋友，体验不同的生活方式。举例来说，很多留学生在第一年就学会了规划开支、应对文化差异。

**主体段 3**：Admittedly, there are some drawbacks. The main disadvantage is the high cost, as tuition fees and living expenses in many countries are very expensive. Some students may also feel lonely at the beginning. However, these problems can be addressed. Students can apply for scholarships or work part-time to ease the financial burden, and most universities run orientation and buddy programmes that help newcomers settle in.
**译**：诚然，留学也有一些缺点。主要的缺点是花费高，很多国家的学费和生活费都很贵。有些学生刚开始也会感到孤独。不过这些问题都有办法缓解：学生可以申请奖学金或做兼职来减轻经济负担，多数学校也会安排迎新和伙伴计划，帮助新生更快适应。

**结论**：In conclusion, although studying abroad may bring some problems such as financial pressure and loneliness, I believe its benefits outweigh its drawbacks, because it can provide a better education and help young people grow up.
**译**：总之，尽管留学可能带来经济压力、孤独等问题，我认为它利大于弊，因为它能提供更好的教育，并帮助年轻人成长。

## 注意

- outweigh 题一定要写出“哪边更大”，开头和结论各说一次。
- 让步段不能只列坏处，要接一句 However，说明坏处为什么比较小。
- 只问 *What are the advantages and disadvantages* 时，利一段、弊一段即可，结构见 [2-5 Two-part questions](<2-5 Two-part questions.md>)。
- 同一个留学话题的 Positive or negative 写法，见原作者的 [2-4 Positive & Negative](<../../../原作者笔记/作文/做题技巧/2-4 Positive & Negative.md>)，可以对照着看两种问法的区别。
