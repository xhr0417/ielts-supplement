# 2-3 Reasons & Solutions（原因 + 解决方法）

## 题型识别

题目问“为什么会这样”和“可以怎么解决”。典型问法：

- What are the causes of … ? What solutions can be taken?
- Why does … happen, and how can it be solved?
- What problems does … cause, and what measures can be taken?

## 结构选择

两种常见写法，选一种即可：

| 结构 | 主体段 1 | 主体段 2 | 适合 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A：分开写 | 原因（2 个） | 解决办法（2 个） | 两个问题都很明确时 |
| B：成对写 | 原因 1 + 对应的解决办法 | 原因 2 + 对应的解决办法 | 原因和解决办法能一一对应时 |

## 开头模板

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| There is no doubt that … has become a serious problem in recent years. | 毫无疑问，……近年来已成为一个严重的问题。 |
| This essay will examine the main causes of … and suggest some possible solutions. | 本文将分析……的主要原因，并提出一些可能的解决办法。 |

## 原因模板

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The main reason for this is that …. | 主要原因是…… |
| One major cause is …. | 一个主要原因是…… |
| This is largely because …. | 这在很大程度上是因为…… |
| Another factor contributing to this is …. | 促成这一现象的另一个因素是…… |
| As a result, …. | 因此，…… |

## 解决模板

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The government should …. | 政府应该…… |
| One effective measure is to …. | 一个有效的办法是…… |
| Individuals can …. | 个人可以…… |
| It is essential to …. | 必须…… |
| Both … and … should work together to …. | ……和……应该共同努力，以…… |

## 结论模板

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| In conclusion, … is mainly caused by … and can be solved by …. | 总之，……主要由……导致，可以通过……解决。 |
| To sum up, … should be addressed through …. | 总而言之，……应该通过……来解决。 |

## 示例（自拟题目）

**题目**：Many young people spend too much time on their phones. What are the causes, and what solutions can be taken?

**开头**：Nowadays, many young people spend a great deal of time on their phones, which has raised concern in society. This essay will examine the main causes of this habit and suggest some possible solutions.
**译**：如今，许多年轻人花大量时间玩手机，这引起了社会的关注。本文将分析这一习惯的主要原因，并提出一些可能的解决办法。

**原因段**：The main reason is that smartphones offer endless entertainment, such as short videos and games, which are designed to attract users' attention. Another factor is that many young people feel lonely or stressed and turn to their phones for comfort. For example, after a long day at school, scrolling through social media may seem like the easiest way to relax.
**译**：主要原因是智能手机提供了无穷无尽的娱乐内容，比如短视频和游戏，它们本身就是为了吸引用户注意力而设计的。另一个因素是许多年轻人感到孤独或压力大，于是通过手机寻求安慰。例如，放学后刷社交媒体似乎是最轻松的放松方式。

**解决段**：To tackle this problem, schools and families should work together to set reasonable rules on screen time. In addition, young people can develop healthier habits, such as doing sports or reading books, to replace the time spent on their phones.
**译**：要解决这个问题，学校和家庭应该共同制定合理的屏幕使用时间规则。此外，年轻人可以培养更健康的习惯，比如运动或阅读，来替代玩手机的时间。

**结论**：In conclusion, excessive phone use among young people is mainly caused by entertainment design and emotional needs, and it can be reduced through clear rules and healthier alternatives.
**译**：总之，年轻人过度用手机主要源于娱乐设计和情感需求，可以通过明确的规则和更健康的替代活动来减少。

## 注意

- 每个原因和解决办法都要有解释（为什么、怎么做）和例子，只写一句话会显得单薄。
- 解决办法要具体、可执行，少用 “we should do more” 这类空话。
- 题目问原因时，段落里先给出原因，再展开说明。
