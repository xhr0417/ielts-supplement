## 一、主稿：#30 省钱买想要的东西（Describe a time when you saved money to buy something special）

### 1. 想买什么及原因（What & Why）
I'd like to talk about the time I saved money last year to buy ==a digital tablet with a stylus==. As a student, I had to read dozens of PDF papers and take lots of notes every week. Printing them out was heavy and wasteful, so I really wanted a tablet to ==go paperless for my studies==, without asking my parents for extra money.

我想讲讲去年我自己攒钱买一台带手写笔的平板电脑的经历。作为学生，我每周要读几十篇 PDF 文献、记大量笔记。全部打印出来又重又浪费，所以我很想买台平板实现无纸化学习，同时不想找父母额外要钱。

### 2. 怎么省钱的（How you saved money）
The tablet cost around 4,000 yuan, so it took me four months to save enough. First, I ==put aside part of my monthly allowance== as soon as I received it. Second, I ==cut down on unnecessary spending==—for example, I ==cooked meals at home instead of ordering takeaway== and stopped buying milk tea every day. On top of that, I ==did a weekend part-time tutoring job== to earn some extra cash.

那台平板大约 4000 元，我攒了四个月。首先，每个月生活费一到账我就先存起一部分；其次，我削减了不必要的开销，比如自己做饭代替点外卖、戒掉每天喝奶茶的习惯；此外，我还利用周末兼职做家教赚了一点外快。

### 3. 买到后的感受（How you felt）
When I finally walked into the store and paid for it with my own money, I felt ==a strong sense of achievement==. Because I worked hard to save for it, I cherish this tablet much more than gifts given by others, and I use it every single day for my exam preparation.

当我最终走进店里用自己攒的钱买下它时，我感到了强烈的成就感。正因为是辛苦攒钱买的，我比别人送的礼物更加珍惜它，现在每天备考都离不开它。

## 二、同素材切换到另外 2 张题卡（怎么改重心）

### 切换为 #22 想拥有的科技产品，不能是手机（Describe a piece of technology, not a phone, that you would like to own）
- **开头时态改为现在/将来**：I'd like to talk about ==a digital tablet with a stylus==—specifically an e-ink tablet—that I really want to own in the near future.
- **重心调整**：不讲“已经买到手”，重点讲**它的功能、怎么帮到你、为什么现在还没买**：
  > Unlike a smartphone with a small screen and constant social media notifications, an e-ink tablet is designed for reading PDFs and taking handwritten notes, and its screen doesn't hurt the eyes after long hours of reading. It would help me ==go paperless for my studies== and stay focused. I haven't bought it yet because it's a bit pricey for a student, so I'm currently ==putting aside part of my monthly allowance== to buy it next month.

### 切换为 #34 在商店得到好的服务（Describe a time when you received good service in a shop/store）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about a time when I received fantastic service at an electronics store in my city last year when I went to buy a tablet.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**店员具体怎么帮你的**：
  > At that time, I couldn't decide which model suited my budget. A ==patient and knowledgeable staff== member named Kevin didn't push me to buy the most expensive one. Instead, he asked about my study needs, ==walked me through the features== of two different models, and even ==helped me apply for a student discount==, which saved me 400 yuan. His honest and warm attitude made me feel really respected.
