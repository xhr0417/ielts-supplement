### 1. 一见且想继续了解的人 `大陆新题` `近期高频`

*Describe a person you only met once recently and want to know more about*

其他叫法：想要了解更多的人

You should say:

- Who he/she is
- When you met him/her

**思路 / 串题**：旅行或活动里只见过一次的人，比如在阳朔民宿遇到的攀岩者。重点讲“为什么想多了解”：他做的事你也想做。

### 13. 擅长做手工的人

*Describe a person who is good at making things by hand*

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What he/she makes
- What materials or technologies he/she uses
