## 一、主稿：#41 在公共场所丢东西（Describe an occasion when you lost something in public）

### 1. 丢了什么、在哪丢的（What & Where）
I'd like to talk about an occasion last winter when I lost ==my wireless earbuds and charging case== on the subway. It was a Friday evening during rush hour, and I was ==in a rush to catch the subway== home from university.

我想讲讲去年冬天我在地铁上弄丢无线耳机和充电盒的经历。那是一个周五晚高峰，我正急着从学校赶地铁回家。

### 2. 怎么丢的、发现后做了什么（How you lost it & What you did）
When I squeezed onto the crowded train, my earbuds ==slipped out of my coat pocket== without me noticing. I didn't realize they were gone until I got off the train and wanted to listen to music. At that moment, ==my mind went blank== because I had saved up for months to buy them. I ==retraced my steps immediately== to the platform and ==used the "Find My" app on my phone== to check their location. Then I hurried to the service desk and ==asked the station staff== for help.

当我挤上拥挤的地铁车厢时，耳机不知不觉从我大衣口袋里滑了出来。直到下车想听歌时，我才发现耳机不见了。那一刻我脑子一片空白，因为那是我攒了好几个月钱才买的。我立刻返回站台寻找，并用手机上的“查找”软件查看定位，接着赶紧去服务台向工作人员求助。

### 3. 结果与感受（Result & How you felt）
Luckily, the staff checked their system and told me that a kind passenger had picked up the case on the train and ==turned it in to the lost-and-found office== at the next station. When I got my earbuds back half an hour later, I ==breathed a huge sigh of relief== and felt really grateful to that stranger. Since then, I've developed the habit of ==zipping up my pockets== whenever I'm in a crowded place.

幸运的是，工作人员查了系统后告诉我，有一位好心乘客在车厢里捡到了耳机盒，并交到了下一站的失物招领处。半小时后拿回耳机时，我长舒了一口气，非常感激那位陌生人。从那以后，我养成了在人多的地方随手拉好口袋拉链的习惯。
