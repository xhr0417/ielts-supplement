## 一、主稿 A：#27 读起来开心的书（Describe an exciting book that you enjoyed reading）

### 1. 书名与何时读的（What & When）
I'd like to talk about an exciting book I read three months ago. It's ==a biography of Eliud Kipchoge==, the legendary marathon runner who broke the two-hour marathon barrier. A close friend recommended it to me when I was feeling stressed about my exams.

我想聊一本三个月前读过、让我非常开心的书——马拉松传奇运动员基普乔格的传记（他曾突破人类马拉松两小时大关）。那是我在备考压力很大时，一位好朋友推荐给我的。

### 2. 书的内容（What it is about）
Instead of listing dry facts, the book is ==written in a vivid and inspiring way==. It tells the story of how Kipchoge grew up in a small village in Kenya, how he trained in simple conditions for decades, and how he stayed calm under huge pressure during the Vienna race. It was such ==a page-turner== that I ==couldn't put it down== and finished it over a single weekend.

这本书没有堆砌枯燥的资料，而是写得生动又励志。它讲述了基普乔格如何在肯尼亚小村庄长大、几十年如一日在简陋条件下训练，以及在维也纳破二挑战赛中如何顶住巨大压力保持冷静。整本书引人入胜，我读得爱不释手，一个周末就看完了。

### 3. 为什么喜欢读（Why you enjoyed it）
I really enjoyed reading this book because it ==taught me the power of self-discipline== and patience. Whenever I feel tired or anxious during my IELTS preparation, I think of his famous quote, "No human is limited," which helps me ==relieve my stress== and keep going.

我非常喜欢这本书，因为它教会了我自律和耐心的力量。每当我在雅思备考中感到疲惫或焦虑时，想起他那句“人类没有极限”，就能缓解压力、继续坚持下去。

## 二、主稿 B：#26 想送朋友的礼物（Describe a gift you would like to buy for your friend）

### 1. 送给谁、送什么（Who & What gift）
I'd like to buy ==a special gift== for my best friend Hao next month for his birthday: ==a hardcover edition of this sports biography== paired with a lightweight running cap.

下个月我好朋友浩过生日，我想买一份特别的礼物送给他：这本精装版运动传记，再配一顶轻便的跑步帽。

### 2. 为什么选这份礼物（Why you chose it）
The reason I picked this gift is that Hao is a big fan of long-distance running. He ==goes for a morning run every day== and is currently training to ==prepare for his first half-marathon== this winter. Rather than buying something flashy that he might never use, I want to give him something both ==practical and meaningful==. The cap will keep the sweat out of his eyes during training, and the book will encourage him when he feels tired.

我选这份礼物是因为浩非常热爱长跑，每天坚持晨跑，最近正在备战今年冬天的人生首场半程马拉松。与其买华而不实的摆件，我更想送他既实用又有意义的东西：跑步帽能在训练时遮阳吸汗，而那本书能在他跑累的时候给他打气。

### 3. 打算怎么送及感受（How you feel）
I plan to ==write a handwritten note on the flyleaf== of the book to wish him luck in his race. I'm sure he will be thrilled when he opens it, because it shows how well I know his hobbies.

我打算在书的扉页上亲手写一段寄语，祝他比赛顺利。我相信他拆开礼物时一定会非常开心，因为这份礼物代表我真的很懂他的爱好。
