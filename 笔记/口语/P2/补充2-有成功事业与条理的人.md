## 一、主稿：#3 有成功事业的人（Describe a person you know who has a successful business）

### 1. 人物与认识途径（Who & How you knew her）
I'd like to talk about my cousin's friend, Ms. Chen, who ==runs a successful handmade pottery and coffee studio== in my city. I got to know her two years ago when I went to her shop for a weekend pottery workshop.

我想聊聊我表姐的朋友陈女士，她在我们城市经营一家很成功的手工陶艺与咖啡工作室。两年前我去她店里参加周末陶艺体验课时认识了她。

### 2. 为什么及如何创业（Why & How she started the business）
She used to be a graphic designer at a big company, but she got tired of sitting in front of a computer all day. Since she loved making ceramics in her spare time, she decided to ==turn her passion into a business==. Five years ago, she ==started from scratch== with her own savings, ==rented a small shop in an old alley==, and bought two kilns and a coffee machine.

她以前在一家大公司做平面设计师，但厌倦了整天坐在电脑前。因为业余时间热爱陶艺，她决定把热爱变成事业。五年前，她用自己的积蓄从零起步，在老巷子里租了间小店，买了两台窑炉和一台咖啡机。

### 3. 生意内容与成功原因（What business & Why it is successful）
Today, her studio offers handmade mugs, plates, and weekend DIY classes, and it has expanded to three branches. I think her business is successful for two reasons. First, she ==pays close attention to every detail== and never compromises on quality. Second, she ==shared short videos on social media== showing how each cup is made, which attracted lots of young people through ==word of mouth== and ==built a loyal customer base==.

如今，她的工作室不仅售卖手工马克杯和餐盘，还开设周末手工课，并且已经开到了三家分店。我觉得她成功有两个原因：一是她对每个细节都极其用心，从不敷衍质量；二是她在社交媒体上发短视频展示每个杯子的制作过程，靠口碑吸引了大量年轻人，积累了稳定的客源。

## 二、同素材切换到另外 3 张题卡（怎么改重心）

### 切换为 #7 很有条理的人（Describe a person you know who is very organized）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about Ms. Chen, a studio owner I know who is the most organized person I've ever met.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**她日常怎么规划时间和整理物品**：
  > Since she has to manage custom orders, teach classes, and run social media at the same time, she ==keeps a well-planned schedule== every single day. In her workshop, hundreds of tools and glazes are labelled clearly and put in order. Because she is so organized, she rarely misses a deadline and handles busy weekends calmly.

### 切换为 #11 擅长做手工的人（Describe a person who is good at making things by hand）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about Ms. Chen, who is really good at making ceramics and pottery by hand.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**她做什么手工、用什么材料和步骤**：
  > She ==moulds clay by hand== on a spinning wheel, carves delicate patterns of flowers or mountains on the surface, and then glazes and fires them in a kiln at high temperatures. Every mug she makes is unique and feels warm in the hand, which shows her patience and creativity.

### 切换为 #13 本地名人（Describe a famous person in your local area）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about Ms. Chen, a well-known local craftswoman and entrepreneur in my city.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**她为什么在当地出名、为社区做了什么**：
  > She became famous locally after her traditional pottery videos went viral online and were reported by our city's TV station. Besides running her studio, she often holds free craft classes for community elders and children, which makes her widely respected in our city.
