## 一、主稿：#24 特别场合吃的食物（Describe a food that people eat on special occasions/events）

### 1. 什么食物与什么场合吃（What food & What occasion）
I'd like to talk about ==homemade dumplings, or Jiaozi==, which people in northern China always eat on special occasions, especially on ==Chinese New Year's Eve== and family reunions.

我想聊一聊手工水饺，这是中国北方人在特殊场合——尤其是除夕夜和家庭团聚时必吃的食物。

### 2. 怎么做、怎么吃的（How it is made and eaten）
Making dumplings on New Year's Eve is a family activity, unlike buying frozen dumplings from the supermarket. All family members ==gather around the dining table==: my grandpa ==makes the dough==, my dad ==chops the fillings==—usually pork, shrimp, and Chinese cabbage—and the rest of us roll the wrappers and fold the dumplings together. My grandma also likes to ==hide a clean coin inside one dumpling==. Whoever bites into that special dumpling is believed to have the best luck in the coming year.

和超市买的速冻饺子不同，除夕夜包饺子是一项全家参与的活动。全家人围坐在餐桌旁：爷爷和面，爸爸调猪肉白菜虾仁馅，其他人一起擀皮、包饺子。奶奶还会特意在一个饺子里包进一枚洗干净的硬币，谁吃到那个饺子，就寓意新的一年运气最好。

### 3. 为什么重要（Why people eat it on special occasions）
People eat dumplings on this special occasion not just because they taste delicious and juicy, but because they are ==a symbol of family reunion and good luck==. Their shape looks like ancient Chinese gold ingots, representing wealth, and making them together really helps ==strengthen family bonds==.

人们在特殊场合吃饺子，不仅因为它鲜美多汁，更因为它是团圆和好运的象征。饺子的形状像中国古代的金元宝，寓意招财进宝，而一家人一起包饺子更能增进感情。

## 二、同素材切换到另外 2 张题卡（怎么改重心）

### 切换为 #37 印象深刻的聚会（Describe a party for a special event that impressed you a lot）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about a family reunion party held at my grandparents' home last Chinese New Year's Eve, which impressed me a lot.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**聚会的人数、活动环节、为什么印象深刻**：
  > Nearly twenty relatives came back from different cities. Instead of just eating at a restaurant, we decorated the living room with red lanterns, made ==homemade dumplings== together, and played card games after dinner. When my little cousin bit into the dumpling with the lucky coin, everyone cheered and laughed. The ==lively and warm atmosphere== made it the most memorable party for me.

### 切换为 #17 拥挤的地方（Describe a crowded place you went to）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about a traditional temple fair and night market I went to with my family during the Spring Festival holiday last year.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**现场为什么那么挤、你在里面做了什么、有什么感受**：
  > Because the weather was nice and everyone was on holiday, the street was ==packed with people shoulder to shoulder==. We had to move slowly in the crowd and wait in line for twenty minutes just to buy local snacks and dumplings. Although it was a bit noisy and tiring, the festive lanterns and cheerful crowd made me feel the lively spirit of the holiday.
