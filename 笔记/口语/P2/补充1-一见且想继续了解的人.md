## 一、主稿：#1 一见且想继续了解的人（Describe a person you only met once recently and want to know more about）

### 1. 人物与偶遇场景（Who & Where）
I'd like to talk about a guy named Lin, whom I ==met by chance== last month during a weekend trip to an old town near my city. He was a university student working as a part-time volunteer guide at a local history museum.

我想聊一个叫林的男生，是我上个月周末去附近古镇玩时偶然认识的。他是一名大学生，在当地历史博物馆做兼职志愿者讲解员。

### 2. 聊了什么（What you talked about）
At that time, I was looking at some old photos of the town, and he came over and ==struck up a conversation== with me. Instead of giving a boring lecture, he ==brought the old stories to life==. We talked for about half an hour about how the town changed over the past century, as well as our favourite books and travel experiences.

当时我正在看古镇的一些老照片，他走过来和我聊了起来。他没有照本宣科，而是把老故事讲得活灵活现。我们聊了大概半小时，从古镇近百年的变迁聊到各自喜欢的书和旅行经历。

### 3. 为什么想进一步了解他（Why you want to know more）
Before I left, we ==exchanged WeChat contacts==, and I really hope to hang out with him again. ==The main reason is that== he is so knowledgeable and ==full of positive energy==. Even though we only met once, I felt like we had a lot in common, and I'd love to ==learn more about his life experiences== and how he studies history in such an interesting way.

离开前我们交换了微信，我非常希望以后能再约他出来。主要原因是他知识面很广，而且充满正能量。虽然只见过一次，但我觉得我们很投缘，很想进一步了解他的经历，看看他是怎么把历史学得这么有趣的。

## 二、同素材切换到另外 3 张题卡（怎么改重心）

### 切换为 #6 快乐的人（Describe a happy person you know）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about my friend Lin, who is the happiest and most optimistic person I know.
- **重心调整**：少讲“只见过一次”，重点讲他**平时如何表现快乐**：
  > He always wears a warm smile and ==has a great sense of humour==. Whenever people around him feel stressed about exams or work, he tells funny stories to cheer them up. Even when he faces difficulties in life, he looks on the bright side instead of complaining, which is why I think he is genuinely happy.

### 切换为 #8 受欢迎的人（Describe a popular person）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about Lin, a volunteer guide and senior student at my university who is super popular among students.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**大家为什么喜欢他**：
  > He ==gets along really well with everyone== because he is always patient, humble, and willing to help newcomers. Whenever he organizes campus tours or history sharing sessions, dozens of students sign up immediately. People enjoy being around him because he listens carefully and makes everyone feel valued.

### 切换为 #10 学习并喜欢历史的人（Describe a person who learns history and loves history）
- **开头改一句**：I'd like to talk about my friend Lin, who is a huge fan of history.
- **重心调整**：重点讲**他怎么学历史、为什么热爱历史**：
  > Rather than just memorizing dates for exams, he learns history by reading biographies, visiting museums, and talking to local elders. He loves history because he believes understanding the past helps us make wiser choices today. Every time we chat, he ==brings the old stories to life==, which makes me admire him a lot.
